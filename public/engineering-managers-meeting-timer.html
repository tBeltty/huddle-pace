Why reviews overrun

1

Context eats the proposal

A long preamble leaves too little time for the part people came for.

2

Objections arrive in the last minute

Reserved time for questions makes them part of the plan.

3

The timeline turns into a root-cause debate

A separate timeline block keeps facts and theories apart.

4

Nobody can show what meetings cost

A 30-day report gives you numbers to bring to planning.

Run it in three steps

  1. 1

    Open the modal

    Open the scheduler from App Home, or type /pace in the channel where the review happens.

  2. 2

    Choose a split

    Pick the RFC or post-mortem split above, or enter your own. The modules must add up to 100.

  3. 3

    Save it as a template

    Tick Save as template and start the next review from it in one click.

Questions from engineering managers

Can I use a different split for each meeting type?

Yes. Save one template per meeting type, for example an RFC review and a post-mortem.

What does the report show?

Timebox adherence and session logs for the period you choose, 30 days by default.

Who can edit a scheduled session?

Only designated speakers, and only while the session has not started.

Does HuddlePace record audio?

No. It records 0% audio and stores no transcripts. It keeps only the timestamps and the agenda modules it needs to run the timer.
