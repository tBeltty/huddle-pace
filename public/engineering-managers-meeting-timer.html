A meeting timer for engineering managers

Protect your team's deep work. HuddlePace keeps design reviews and post-mortems inside the time you set, and shows you how long your calls really take.

Install to Slack

Why overrunning meetings hurt

A review that drifts past its slot eats the focus time your engineers need to close tickets. A visible clock gives the whole team a reason to stay on the topic.

RFC and design review split

For a 30-minute review, run /pace and split the time into three modules:

  • Problem context, 15% (4 min 30 s)
  • Architecture proposal, 50% (15 min)
  • Questions and objections, 35% (10 min 30 s)

Incident post-mortem split

For a 40-minute post-mortem:

  • Incident timeline, 25% (10 min)
  • Root cause, 45% (18 min)
  • Prevention plan, 30% (12 min)

Save either split with Save as template and start the next one in a click.

See where the time goes

/pace report 30 shows timebox adherence and session logs for the last 30 days, so you can back a meeting-load conversation with data.
